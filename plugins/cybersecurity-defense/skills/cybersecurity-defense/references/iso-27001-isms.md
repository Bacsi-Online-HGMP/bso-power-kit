
# ISO 27001 ISMS

**Safety:** guidance only. ISO 27001 certification requires an accredited auditor;
this skill prepares you, it does not certify you.

## When to use
Building an ISMS, preparing for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification or surveillance,
writing a Statement of Applicability, or mapping Annex A controls.

## Core sequence
1. **Context & scope (Cl. 4)** — define ISMS boundary, interested parties, and
   information assets in/out of scope.
2. **Leadership (Cl. 5)** — security policy, roles, management commitment.
3. **Risk management (Cl. 6 / 8)** — risk assessment methodology, risk register,
   risk treatment plan; select controls to treat each risk.
4. **Statement of Applicability** — list all Annex A controls, mark
   applicable/not-applicable with justification, and link to treatments.
5. **Annex A:2022 (93 controls, 4 themes)** — Organizational (37), People (8),
   Physical (14), Technological (34). Implement those in your SoA.
6. **Operate (Cl. 7–9)** — competence/awareness, documented information,
   monitoring & measurement, internal audit, management review.
7. **Improve (Cl. 10)** — nonconformities, corrective actions, continual improvement.
8. **Certification** — Stage 1 (documentation) → Stage 2 (implementation) →
   surveillance audits.

## Key artifacts to maintain
ISMS scope, security policy, risk methodology + register + treatment plan, SoA,
control evidence, internal-audit program, management-review minutes, corrective actions.

## Tip
Map Annex A:2022 controls to what you already run (e.g. NIST CSF, existing IT
controls) to avoid duplicate work; the SoA is the spine auditors follow.
